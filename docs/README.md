# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

This folder contains OctoAcme's project management processes and guidance. The lifecycle starts by validating a project need, aligning stakeholders, and assigning clear roles; teams then plan scope, milestones, dependencies, and a prioritized backlog. During execution, they deliver and test in small increments, track progress and blockers, and communicate status and risks. Releases are verified and announced, and retrospectives turn lessons into owned, measurable improvements.

## Process documents

- [Project Management Overview](octoacme-project-management-overview.md) — principles, roles, artifacts, lifecycle, and communication cadence.
- [Project Initiation](octoacme-project-initiation.md) — validate proposals, align stakeholders, and decide whether to proceed to planning.
- [Project Planning](octoacme-project-planning.md) — shape scope, backlog, estimates, milestones, and dependencies.
- [Execution and Tracking](octoacme-execution-and-tracking.md) — manage delivery workflows, quality, progress, and blocker escalation.
- [Risks and Communication](octoacme-risks-and-communication.md) — maintain risks and dependencies, communicate status, and escalate issues.
- [Release and Deployment](octoacme-release-and-deployment.md) — prepare, deploy, verify, announce, and roll back releases when needed.
- [Retrospective and Continuous Improvement](octoacme-retrospective-and-continuous-improvement.md) — capture learnings and track improvement actions.
- [Roles and Personas](octoacme-roles-and-personas.md) — responsibilities and communication patterns for project roles.
